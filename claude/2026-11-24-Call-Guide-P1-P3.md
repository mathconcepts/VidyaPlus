# Call guide: 8 discovery calls (4 working professionals, 4 late starters)

Plan T16 · Feature SSOT open questions · Mom Test rules.
**Giri runs every call. Outreach stays human (constraint 13). Notes carry a code (C1–C8), never a name or a phone number (constraint 12). Every caller is 18+ (constraint 11).**

## Purpose

Settle the 5 SSOT open questions with past behaviour rather than opinions. The same questions go to both groups, so the answers can be compared directly. Whichever group shows the stronger pull becomes the first customer (decision, 24 Nov).

| # | Question | What it settles |
|---|---|---|
| Q-A | Do late starters want the whole syllabus, or only what to skip? | F8 "complete section" (out of line for P1 today) |
| Q-B | Would anyone pay for verified keys, or only for practice plus a plan? | F23 tiers, F24 price |
| Q-C | Do working professionals really have 30-minute slots, and when? | P3 column, F16 Today card |
| Q-D | Does NAT come up without being asked? | F17 (no evidence today) |
| Q-E | Have wrong keys or wrong AI answers cost them time or marks? | F2, F6, F7, F20 (the trust wedge) |

## Who (screener)

- **P3, working professional:** has a full-time job and is preparing for GATE EC 2027 (or wants to).
- **P1, late starter:** a final-year EC student who started GATE prep in the last 3 months, or hasn't started yet.

Both groups must be 18+ and preparing for GATE EC 2027, not just curious. Skip anyone Giri has already pitched Vidhya to.

## Rules for the call (15 minutes)

- Ask about the **last time** something happened, not what they would do.
- **No pitching** until the last two minutes, and only if they ask. Don't mention Vidhya's features while you're learning.
- Don't name an answer for them ("would a marks planner help?"). Ask what they did instead.
- Any "I would…" or "that's a great idea" is noise. Follow up with "When did you last…?"
- Take notes as quotes, word for word.

## Script

**Open (1 min).** "Thanks for the time. I'm trying to understand how people actually prepare for GATE EC maths. I'm not selling anything today. Tell me about where your prep is right now."

**Their week (4 min) — Q-C**
1. "Walk me through the last day you studied. When did you start, and what stopped you?"
2. "How long was the longest uninterrupted block last week? When was it?"
3. "What did you study in the last short gap you had, say under 30 minutes?"

**The syllabus (4 min) — Q-A, Q-D**
4. "How did you decide which Engineering Maths topics to do first? Who or what told you?"
5. "Have you dropped a topic on purpose? Which one, and why?"
6. "Tell me about the last mock or PYQ set you did. Which questions did you lose marks on?" *(Listen for NAT. Don't prompt for it.)*

**Trust (3 min) — Q-E**
7. "When did you last find a wrong answer key or a wrong solution? What did you do next?"
8. "Have you used an AI tool for a maths doubt? What happened the last time?"

**Money (2 min) — Q-B**
9. "What have you paid for GATE prep so far? What was it, how much, and was it worth it?"
10. "Is there anything you stopped paying for? Why?"

**Close (1 min).** "Who else is preparing whom I should talk to?" If they ask what you're building, take 2 minutes and offer the free diagnostic link. Make no promises.
