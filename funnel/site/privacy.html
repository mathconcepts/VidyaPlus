Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Short version

You can take the diagnostic without giving us anything. We only store a Telegram username if you type it in to unlock your plan, and we use it only to send that plan and Vidhya updates you asked for.

What we collect

  • Anonymous completion record when you finish the diagnostic: your marks, per-topic marks, how you prepare (if you answered), time taken, and a random referral code. No name, phone or username.
  • If you unlock your plan: your Telegram username, your answers and score, and confirmation that you are 18+.
  • JEE waitlist: your Telegram username and the 18+/parent confirmation.
  • Analytics: if enabled, Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not track you across sites.

Answers you are still working on are kept only in your own browser (localStorage) until you finish.

Where it is stored

In a private Google Sheet owned by the founder, reached through a Google Apps Script. The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

Who sees it

Only the founder. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it to train AI models.

How long

Until you ask us to delete it, or 12 months after your last activity, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time via the contact page. We reply within 7 days. This follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

Vidhya is for people aged 18 and over. The JEE waitlist accepts a parent signing up on behalf of their child. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.
