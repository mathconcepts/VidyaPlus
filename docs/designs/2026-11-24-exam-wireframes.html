/exam · wireframes v2

Updated after the design review on 24 Nov. Uses the Clarity tokens (DESIGN-SYSTEM.md). Each screen notes the decision (D3–D18) behind it. Phone frames are 360 × 740 and the laptop frame is 1280 wide, scaled down. Numbers are placeholders.

Apple (Clarity) is the base: one focal block per screen, type-led, a 17px floor, and colour only for meaning. From Netflix: one obvious "continue" row and one hero action. From Tesla: one gauge read at a glance, and every other screen one tap away. From Microsoft Fluent: adapt the layout to the window size, with a visible focus ring, full keyboard paths and MathML for screen readers.
